Part 2 of 6

📦 Lists 101: the three traps

Before we hunt, learn the rules.

Explain it like I'm 6 A list is a row of numbered boxes. The first box is number 0, not 1. When you ask for a slice marks[1:3], you get boxes 1 and 2 — the stop number is not included. It's like saying "give me pages 1 up to but not including 3".
Trap 1 · Index from 0
First item is [0]. Last item of length-5 list is [4]. [5] crashes: IndexError.
Trap 2 · Slice excludes stop
[1:3] gives items at 1 and 2. To include item 3, you must write [1:4].
Trap 3 · Mutating while looping
If you remove() inside a for loop, the indices shift and items get skipped. Use a copy.

Case 2: The shrinking list

This program tries to remove every even mark. The author expected [72, 38, 64] removed, leaving [56, 91].

Explain it like I'm 6 Picture 5 people in a queue, numbered 0 to 4. You walk down the line saying "you're out". But when person 1 leaves, person 2 becomes the new number 1 — and you've already walked past them. They never get checked. That's what remove() in a for loop does.

Fill in the trace

Trace for m in marks: — Python walks the positions, not the values. Write the list state after each remove() that actually happens.

Stepmmarks after

There are only 5 steps and not all of them remove. This is the trap.

Final list:

Cause: for m in marks: walks indices 0,1,2,3,4 in order. Removing item at index 1 shifts everything left — so the item that was at index 2 is now at index 1, and the loop moves on to index 2, skipping it.
Result: [72, 91, 64] — not [56, 91].
Fix: loop over a copy: for m in marks[:]: — or build a new list with a comprehension.

Case report

Final combined average after fixes:

Bug 1 (crash): line 6 loops for i in range(1, 6) — that's indices 1..5. But indices are 0..4, so marks[5] crashes.
Bug 2 (wrong answer): even after fixing the range, starting at 1 skips index 0 — the first mark is never counted. The sum is short by 56, but you still divide by 5.
Fix: for i in range(len(marks)): — starts at 0, stops before length.
Result: Average: 64.2 ✔
